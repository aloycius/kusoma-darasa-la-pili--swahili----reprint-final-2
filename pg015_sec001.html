Mazingira

Leo ninawaeleza, mazingira kukumbuka,
Miti tunaitatiza, madhara yanatufika,
Asili tunapoteza, mazingira haribika,
Tuungane kwa pamoja, tuyatunze mazingira.
Tuyatunze mazingira, kuziepuka gharama,
Tuyaepuke madhara, kama ukame sikia,
Tapata mvua bora, mazao kujilimia,
Tuungane kwa pamoja, tuyatunze mazingira.
